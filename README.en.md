# L.I.A — Voice Assistant for Star Citizen

L.I.A is a Windows desktop app for voice and keyboard control in Star Citizen. It recognizes commands, presses assigned keys, speaks responses, and reacts to game events exposed by the game.

## 🎮 Controls and interaction

✅ **More than 90 built-in commands** for ships, ground vehicles, and in-game interaction.

✅ Control landing gear, power, engines, shields, weapons, power distribution, flight modes, targets, airlocks, the map, mobiGlas, radar, and ship self-destruct.

✅ Assistant phrases include introductions, basic and advanced help, stop playback, and playful replies.

✅ Assign keys and modifiers to commands, with repeat, double-press, and hold options.

✅ For paired state-changing commands, one key can be assigned to both actions—for example, deploying and retracting landing gear. L.I.A reads the current state and selects the appropriate action for gear, power, engines, weapons, and shields.

✅ Choose continuous listening with an in-app toggle, or Push-to-Talk: the microphone listens only while the assigned key is held.

### ✨ Your own commands

🌟 Create commands for the way you play: set a recognition phrase and synonyms, assign a key, and give L.I.A. a custom spoken reply.

✅ Custom command settings are saved separately for Ukrainian, English, and German, so switching languages keeps each set of commands intact.

## 🛰️ In-game assistance

L.I.A adapts available commands to the current in-game situation and announces selected game events.

✅ Detects whether the player is in a ship or ground vehicle and limits vehicle commands to the current context.

✅ Shows the status of power, engines, weapons, shields, coolers, and landing gear.

✅ Plays voice warnings for incoming missile threats and collision hazards.

✅ Reacts to an ATC docking-clearance response.

✅ Announces disabled and destroyed ships, ground vehicles, and satellites, plus events involving pilots, players, NPCs, animals, and worms. It distinguishes player ships from NPC ships. Kill-streak comments are available for 10 targets, 5 ships, or 5 animals.

✅ Plays localized The Collector dialogue in Ukrainian and German. English dialogue is not duplicated because the game already voices those lines.

✅ Provides spoken information about Star Citizen locations, including planets and moons.

⚙️ Missile, collision, docking, kill-event, streak-comment, and dialogue notifications can be enabled or disabled separately in settings.

## 🎙️ Languages, speech, and personalization

ℹ️ The interface and built-in commands are available in Ukrainian, English, and German. Choose a language on first launch; Ukrainian is the default. The language can also be changed without restarting the app. Ukrainian is L.I.A.’s primary language; its spoken replies are synthesized and processed, and the range of available lines continues to grow.

✅ English uses the Amy voice; German uses Eva K.

🚧 The English and German voices are not final and will be refined further.

✅ Pronunciation and pause rules can be applied to custom spoken responses.

## ⚙️ Settings, installation, and updates

✅ Light and dark themes, volume control, key-press sounds, and assistant voice messages.

✅ Separate controls for game-event notifications and handlers.

✅ Minimize the app to the system tray.

✅ The installer can create Desktop and Start menu shortcuts. During uninstall, users can remove all L.I.A data or keep their personal settings.

✅ The app announces available updates and automatically downloads and launches their installer.


## 🚧 Current status

🚧 Full mission voice-over is in development.

🚫 Hyperjump voice announcements and synchronization are not supported in the current version.

🚧 English and German voices are still being refined.
